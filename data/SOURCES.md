## How the scores work

Each factor is a 1–10 score that ranks the neighbourhood against Toronto's other 157 (percentile rank: 10 = best in the city, 1 = lowest). They are comparisons, not absolute grades.

- **School quality:** average % of students meeting the provincial standard (Grade 3 and 6 reading, writing, math; Grade 9 math; Grade 10 literacy) across schools inside or within 1 km of the neighbourhood.
- **Safety:** weighted crime rate per 100,000 residents (assault, robbery, break and enter, auto theft, theft over, shootings, homicides), averaged over the last 3 years. Downtown areas with many visitors and few residents score lower on a per-resident basis.
- **Transit access:** scheduled TTC stop visits per km², plus a bonus if a subway station is within 800 m.
- **Parks & green space:** share of the neighbourhood's area that is green space.
- **Walkability:** shops, groceries, pharmacies, cafés and restaurants per km².
- **Restaurants & nightlife:** restaurants and bars per km².
- **Peace & quiet:** the inverse of population density and nightlife.
- **Family-friendly:** share of residents under 15 (45%), plus safety, schools and parks.
- **Price:** 2021 Census median owner-estimated value of all owned homes, including condos. It will be replaced by live IDX listing prices.

Scores are for guidance only and are not a guarantee about any home or school.
