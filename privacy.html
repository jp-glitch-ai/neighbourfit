Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

NeighbourhoodFit is operated by Jason Pittelli, REALTOR®, Bosley Real Estate Ltd., Brokerage ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect on this site, why, and your choices. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

What we collect

  • What you give us: your first name, email, and optionally your phone number and best time to call.
  • Your quiz answers: priorities, budget range, timing, what you're looking to do, and your top neighbourhood matches.
  • How you found us: the campaign or ad you came from (for example a Google Ads click ID).

Why we use it

  • To show and send you your neighbourhood report.
  • To follow up about your home search or investment, by email or, if you asked, by phone.
  • To send occasional Toronto market insights, only if you ticked the consent box. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • To measure which ads work, so we spend less showing ads to people they don't help.

Where it's stored and who sees it

Your details are stored in a private Google Sheet and seen only by Jason Pittelli. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. Service providers that help run the site (Google for storage, email and advertising measurement; GitHub for hosting) process it on our behalf and may store data outside Canada.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google's advertising tag to record when someone who clicked an ad completes the quiz. It doesn't use your answers for ad targeting. You can manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

How long we keep it

We keep lead information for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete it, unless you become a client, in which case brokerage record-keeping rules apply.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent at any time. Contact Jason through homeistoronto.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

About the neighbourhood scores

Scores compare Toronto's 158 official neighbourhoods using public open data (see sources). They are for guidance only and are not a guarantee of school placement, safety, or property value.
